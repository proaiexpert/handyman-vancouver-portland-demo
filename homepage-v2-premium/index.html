FAQ

Questions before you send a request

Can I send photos first?

Yes. Photos help clarify the issue before the next step is confirmed. A wide photo and a close-up are usually most helpful.

Do you give exact prices online?

Not every request can be priced from photos alone. Final scope depends on project type, location, access, materials, and condition.

What repair types can be requested?

Common requests include wall touch-ups, trim details, door-related fixes, small installs, painting touch-ups, and punch-list maintenance.

Can several small repairs be grouped together?

Yes. A punch-list request can include several small items so the overall scope can be reviewed together.

Do you handle plumbing or electrical?

Regulated work may require licensed trades. Requests involving plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, or structural work should be reviewed carefully before any next step.

What areas do you cover?

Request review is centered around Vancouver, WA and nearby Clark County areas. Portland-area requests can be reviewed by location and project scope.

What happens after I submit a request?

The project details are reviewed. The next step may be a follow-up question, estimate path, scheduling discussion, referral, or scope clarification.
