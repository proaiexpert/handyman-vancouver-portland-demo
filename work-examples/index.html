Sample Scenarios

Sample repair and install scenarios

These example project types show how a local handyman site can organize and present common repair and install requests.

Trim & Baseboard

Trim & Baseboard Refresh

Cleaner edges and finish details around walls, trim, and baseboards. Gaps, caulk lines, and paint-edge details reviewed by photos and scope.

Fixture Detail

Kitchen Faucet Detail

Small sink and fixture details reviewed by access, fit, and finish. Includes faucet swap, supply-line connection, and under-sink access.

Door & Hardware

Door & Hardware Details

Handles, hinges, alignment, and visible door details reviewed together. Latches, strikes, and hardware finish all covered in one request.

Bathroom Detail

Bathroom Detail Touch-Up

Caulk lines, grout detail, fixture alignment, and surface touch-ups in bathrooms. Photos of the full area and close-up details help clarify scope.

Entry & Exterior

Entry Door & Exterior Trim

Front door hardware, exterior trim alignment, weatherstrip condition, and threshold detail. Access and door swing direction noted in request.

Appliance Connection

Washer & Dryer Connection

Connection review, access, spacing, vent or hose visibility. Photos of the laundry area, connections, and available clearance help scope the request.

TV & Wall Mount

TV Wall Mount Install

TV bracket mounting, basic placement review, wall type and access check. Stud location, cable routing preference, and wall finish all noted in request.

Fixture Install

Ceiling Light Fixture Install

Fixture replacement review, ceiling access, existing box and wiring visibility. Photos of the fixture, junction box, and ceiling area help clarify scope.
