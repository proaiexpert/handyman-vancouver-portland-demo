SAMPLE SCENARIOS

Sample repair and install scenarios

These example project types show how a local handyman site can organize and present common repair and install requests.

Trim & Trim Detail

Trim & Baseboard Refresh

Cleaner edges and finish details around walls, trim, and baseboards. Gaps, caulk lines, and paint-edge details reviewed by photos and scope.

Fixture Detail

Kitchen Faucet Detail

Small sink and fixture details reviewed by access, fit, and finish. Includes faucet swap, supply-line connection, and under-sink access.

Door & Hardware

Door & Hardware Details

Handles, hinges, alignment, and visible door details reviewed together. Latches, strikes, and hardware finish all covered in one request.

Bath Detail

Bathroom Detail Touch-Up

Vanity, fixture, and finish details reviewed for a cleaner bathroom feel. Caulk lines, hardware, and visible surface details covered.

Entry & Exterior

Entry Door & Exterior Trim

Visible entry details reviewed by access, material, and exterior condition. Includes door alignment, exterior trim gaps, and paint-edge detail.

Appliance Install

Washer & Dryer Connection

Laundry setup details reviewed by space, access, and connection needs. Supply lines, drain hose, and level-on-pedestal details included.

TV Mounting

TV Wall Mount Install

Mounting requests reviewed by wall type, stud placement, and room layout. Bracket selection and cable management noted in the request.

Ceiling Fixture

Ceiling Light Fixture Install

Light fixture requests reviewed by ceiling access, existing junction box, and placement. Includes flush-mounts, pendants, and fan installs.

Images and scenarios are used as sample project examples for this demo site. A real client version would use the contractor’s actual project photos, reviews, and service details.
