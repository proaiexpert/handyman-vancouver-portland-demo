SCOPE NOTE

Some work may need a specialist

Some larger electrical, plumbing, roofing, structural, or permit-heavy work may require a licensed trade specialist or contractor. Smaller repair, install, touch-up, and punch-list requests can still be reviewed first.

QUESTIONS

Service category questions

Are these guaranteed services?

No. These are request categories. The next step depends on photos, location, access, materials, project type, and scope.

Can I send several small items together?

Yes. A punch-list request can include several small items when photos and notes are included together.

Do photos help before the next step?

Yes. Photos help clarify the repair area, materials, surface, fixture, and condition before the next step is clarified.

What if I am not sure which category fits?

Choose the closest category or send a quick question with photos and a short description.

Does location affect service review?

Yes. Location can affect access, travel, material planning, timing fit, and whether the request can be reviewed.

What if the work requires electrical, plumbing, roofing, or structural work?

Some larger or permit-heavy work may require a licensed trade specialist or contractor.
