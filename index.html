Can photos provide an exact estimate?
Not always. Photos can clarify condition and scale, while access, materials and hidden conditions may still affect the scope.
Can several small tasks be grouped?
Yes. A punch-list request can organize several smaller items for review together.
What if a licensed specialist may be needed?
The request can be reviewed for fit, but regulated or permit-heavy work may need a licensed trade specialist or another provider.
Is every listed city guaranteed?
No. The city pages organize geographic context; availability is not represented as guaranteed.
