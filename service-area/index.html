HOW IT WORKS

How local repair request review works

Vancouver and Portland area homes vary by building age, access, parking, materials, and project type. A repair request in a newer Vancouver suburb may look different from one in an older Portland neighborhood — access, stairs, parking, and material availability can all affect what details are needed before the next step is clarified.

Nearby city areas including Hillsboro, Beaverton, Gresham, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Oregon City, Camas, and Battle Ground may be reviewed by location and scope. The listed city areas are not a guaranteed service boundary. Availability depends on location, project type, access, materials, and scheduling fit.

Photos, close-ups, access notes, and material details help make the first review more useful. A few clear photos of the repair area, the surface or fixture, and any access details help clarify the project type and what the next step may be.
