## What the website includes

**Homepage**
Clear headline, service summary, CTA buttons, trust sections, service area
preview, and footer — all on the first page a visitor sees.

**Services page**
Organized service categories so customers can find what they need quickly
without reading a long paragraph.

**Work Examples / Project Gallery**
A visual page showing real project types. Replace the sample images with
your own photos — before and after photos work especially well.

**Service Area page**
A hub page that shows the cities and areas you serve, with links to
city-specific pages.

**City / Location pages**
Individual pages for each city or area in your service zone. These help
with local search results when customers search by city.

**Request Estimate page**
A structured intake form — not just "Contact Us." Customers are guided to
share photos, describe the project, note their location and timing, and
provide contact details. Fewer back-and-forth messages before you respond.

**FAQ page**
Answers common questions before customers ask them — what you need before
a visit, what areas you serve, how the estimate process works.

**Mobile layout**
The site is designed to work on phones. Most customers will find you on
a mobile device.

**Phone / text / email CTAs**
Multiple ways for customers to reach you, visible throughout the site.
