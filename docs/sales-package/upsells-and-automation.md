# Upsells and Automation

Practical add-on services and automation options for local-service website clients.

Present these as optional upgrades that can be added at any stage —
not requirements, and not promises of guaranteed results.

---

## 1. Structured estimate request form

**What it is:**
A proper intake form that guides customers to share what you actually
need before reviewing a job.

**Fields:**
- Name (optional)
- Best contact method (phone or email)
- Service type (dropdown or multi-select)
- Project description (free text)
- Location / city
- Photos (file upload or instructions to text)
- Timing preference
- Budget range (optional)

**Options:**
- Google Forms (free, simple)
- Tally (clean design, free tier available)
- Custom HTML form with email notification
- Connected to Google Sheet for logging

**Complexity:** Low

**Value to client:**
Fewer back-and-forth messages. Better quality leads. Estimate reviews
are faster when the information is already organized.

---

## 2. Lead logging (Google Sheet or simple CRM)

**What it is:**
Every estimate request submission is automatically logged in a spreadsheet
or simple CRM so nothing is missed.

**Columns (example):**
| Lead name | Phone | Email | City | Service type | Description | Timing | Status | Date |

**Status options:** New / Contacted / Estimate sent / Booked / Closed / Not a fit

**Options:**
- Google Sheets (free, connected via form)
- Notion database (clean, free tier)
- Airtable (more structured)
- Simple CRM (HubSpot free, Go High Level paid)

**Complexity:** Low to medium

**Value to client:**
Clear view of all incoming leads. Easy to follow up.
No leads fall through the cracks.

---

## 3. Missed-call SMS follow-up

**What it is:**
When a customer calls and nobody answers, an automated SMS is sent
within a few minutes.

**Example message:**
> "Hi, this is [Business name]. Sorry I missed your call. Please
> text a few photos and a short description of the project, and
> I will follow up as soon as I can."

**How it works:**
- Requires a missed-call SMS tool (examples: OpenPhone, Google Voice +
  Zapier, Go High Level, Callrail)
- Message is sent automatically; response is manual

**Complexity:** Low to medium

**Value to client:**
Customers who call and do not get an answer often move to the next
result. This keeps them engaged and gives them a clear next step.

---

## 4. AI email / SMS assistant

**What it is:**
An AI tool that reads incoming estimate requests and drafts a suggested
reply. The business owner reviews the draft and sends it (or edits it).

**What it can do:**
- Draft a professional reply to an estimate request
- Classify the request type (repair, install, punch-list, etc.)
- Ask clarifying questions if key information is missing
- Flag requests that may be out of scope

**What it does NOT do:**
- Send replies automatically without human review
- Make pricing commitments
- Guarantee any outcome

**Complexity:** Medium

**Value to client:**
Faster responses without having to write every reply from scratch.
Useful for businesses receiving 10+ inquiries per week.

---

## 5. Chatbot / site assistant

**What it is:**
A small chat widget on the website that can answer common questions
and collect basic intake information.

**What it can do:**
- Answer FAQ questions (hours, service area, how to request an estimate)
- Collect: name, city, service type, project description
- Route to the estimate form
- Capture contact info for follow-up

**What it does NOT do:**
- Give prices or guarantees
- Make legal or technical commitments
- Replace a real conversation

**Options:**
- Tidio (free tier)
- Crisp (free tier)
- Custom AI assistant (higher complexity)

**Complexity:** Medium to high

**Value to client:**
Answers basic questions 24/7. Captures leads outside business hours.

---

## 6. Review request workflow

**What it is:**
After a job is completed, a message is sent to the client asking them
to leave a Google review.

**Example message:**
> "Hi [Name], thanks for choosing [Business name]. If you had a good
> experience, it would really help us if you left a quick review on
> Google: [link]. Thank you!"

**Important:**
- Only sent to real clients after real completed jobs.
- No fake reviews. No incentivized reviews.
- Message is honest and simple.

**Complexity:** Low

**Value to client:**
Google reviews are one of the most important local trust signals.
Most happy customers do not leave reviews unless asked.

---

## 7. Reporting

**What it is:**
A simple weekly or monthly summary of incoming leads and activity.

**Can include:**
- Total leads received
- Lead source breakdown (website form, call, Google, referral)
- Request type breakdown (what services were requested most)
- Response time average
- Conversion rate (if tracked)

**Options:**
- Manual Google Sheets report
- Automated dashboard (Looker Studio, free)
- Simple weekly email summary

**Complexity:** Low to medium

**Value to client:**
Clear picture of what is working. Easy to justify ongoing investment
in the website and lead system.

---

## Complexity tiers

### Low complexity (quick to set up)
- Google Forms estimate intake
- Google Sheet lead log
- Review request message template
- Basic reporting in Sheets

### Medium complexity
- Missed-call SMS follow-up
- Form-to-Sheet automation (Zapier or native)
- AI draft reply tool
- Simple chatbot (Tidio/Crisp)

### Advanced / high complexity
- Full CRM pipeline (Go High Level, HubSpot)
- AI-powered intake and classification
- Custom chatbot with full FAQ and intake flow
- Multi-channel automation (call, SMS, email, WhatsApp)
- Advanced reporting dashboard

## Notes for Ihor / Примітки для Іхора

> - Починай з простого. Форма + Google Sheet вже набагато краще,
>   ніж нічого. Більшість клієнтів оцінять це.
>
> - Missed-call SMS — це часто "вау-момент" для клієнта.
>   Поясни: "Ви пропустили дзвінок — клієнт отримав SMS і не пішов."
>
> - AI-асистент продавай як допомогу власнику, не як замінника.
>   "Не ти пишеш — тобі пропонують текст, ти перевіряєш і надсилаєш."
>
> - Відгуки (Review workflow) — легко продати як самостійний
>   апгрейд. Багато власників знають, що відгуки важливі, але
>   не просять клієнтів їх залишати. Це проблема, яку ти вирішуєш.
