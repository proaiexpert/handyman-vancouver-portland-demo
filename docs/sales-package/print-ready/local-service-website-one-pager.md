## Structure vs. tools: why not just use a website builder?

DIY builders give you drag-and-drop tools. They do not give you a proven page structure,
local-service positioning, a proper estimate intake flow, or done-for-you content.

| Generic builder | This structure |
|---|---|
| Template pages with no local focus | City-specific pages built for local search |
| Generic contact form | Structured estimate intake with service type, photos, location, timing |
| You figure out what pages to make | Purpose-built page set for service businesses |
| You write all the copy | Content written for you, adapted to your business |
| No lead system | Optional: logging, notifications, follow-up automation |

## Optional lead system

For business owners who want more than a website — a structured way to handle every incoming
request so nothing gets lost.

- **Structured estimate form** — service type, description, city, photos, timing
- **Lead logging** — every submission recorded automatically in Google Sheets or a simple CRM
- **Email notifications** — instant alert when a new estimate request arrives
- **Missed-call SMS** — automatic text sent when a call is not answered
- **AI assistant (optional)** — helps draft replies, classify requests, and collect missing info
