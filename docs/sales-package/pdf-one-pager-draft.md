# Premium Local-Service Website + Estimate Flow

**A cleaner way for homeowners and property clients to understand your services,
see your work, and request an estimate.**

---

## What this is

A done-for-you website structure built specifically for local-service businesses.
Not a generic template. A purpose-built system that organizes your services,
presents your work professionally, shows where you operate, and makes it easy
for customers to send you a proper estimate request.

## What it includes

**Homepage**
Professional first impression with clear headline, service summary, and phone/text/email buttons.

**Services page**
Organized service categories — easy to scan, easier to find.

**Work Examples**
A photo gallery for real project photos. Before and after photos work best.

**Service Area**
A hub page showing which cities and areas you serve.

**City pages**
Individual pages for each city you serve — useful for local search.

**Request Estimate**
A structured form that guides customers to share photos, describe the project,
and provide location and timing. Better than a basic "Contact Us" form.

**FAQ**
Answers common questions before customers ask them — builds trust upfront.

**Mobile layout**
Designed to work well on phones. Most customers will find you on a phone.

**Phone / text / email CTAs**
Multiple clear ways to reach you throughout the site.

**Optional automation**
Lead logging, email alerts, missed-call follow-up, and AI draft replies available as add-ons.
