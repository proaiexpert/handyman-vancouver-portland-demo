## Package 3 — Website + Lead System

**Best for:** A business that wants a website AND a better system for
handling incoming leads — so inquiries are tracked, follow-ups happen
automatically, and nothing falls through the cracks.

### What is included

- Everything in Package 2, plus:

**Estimate form**
- Structured fields (service type, description, location, photos, timing)
- Google Forms, Tally, or custom form option
- Email notification on submission
- Optional auto-reply to client confirming receipt

**Lead logging**
- Google Sheet or simple CRM setup
- Columns: lead name, phone, email, city, service type, notes, timing, status
- Submission data flows automatically to the sheet

**Missed-call follow-up**
- SMS sent automatically after a missed call
- Message example: "Hi, this is [Business name]. Sorry I missed your call.
  Please text photos or a description of the project and I will follow up
  shortly."
- Requires third-party SMS tool (cost may be extra)

**AI email/SMS assistant (optional)**
- Reviews incoming requests
- Drafts a suggested reply (human reviews and sends)
- Classifies request type
- Asks clarifying questions if needed
- Does not make pricing commitments automatically

**Chatbot / site assistant (optional)**
- Answers common FAQ questions
- Collects basic intake (name, city, project type)
- Routes to estimate form
- Does not give prices or make guarantees

**Review request workflow**
- After project completion, sends a message asking for a Google review
- No fake reviews. Only real requests to real clients.

**Basic reporting**
- Weekly or monthly lead summary
- Source tracking (how did they find you)
- Request categories (what type of work is most requested)

### What is NOT included

- Paid ads setup or management
- Ongoing content creation
- Ongoing SEO management
- Advanced CRM with full pipeline management (available as a separate service)

### What the client must provide

- Everything in Packages 1 and 2
- Preferred communication channel (email, SMS, WhatsApp, etc.)
- Access to a phone system that supports missed-call SMS (or willingness to
  set one up)
- Approval to test and verify form submissions and notifications

- Prices above are placeholders. Set real prices based on your cost,
  time, market rates, and client budget.
- Do not promise guaranteed Google rankings. SEO takes time and
  depends on competition, reviews, links, and content.
- Do not promise guaranteed lead volume or conversion rates.
- All automation is presented as a tool to support the business owner,
  not a replacement for their judgment.
