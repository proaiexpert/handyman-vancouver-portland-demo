# Client Objection Handling

Prepared responses to common objections.
Use these as a guide — adapt to the conversation.

---

## Objection 1: "I already have a website."

**Response:**
> "That is a good starting point. Having a website and having a website
> that works well for your business are not always the same thing.
> What I can show you is a specific structure built for local-service
> businesses — with organized services, project examples, city pages
> for local search, and a better estimate request flow. Whether or not
> it is an improvement depends on what you currently have. Happy to
> take a look and give you an honest opinion."

**What to avoid:**
- Do not say "your site is bad" or "your site doesn't work."
- Do not insult the current site unprompted.
- Offer to look at it together and give honest, specific feedback.

---

## Objection 2: "I can use Wix / Squarespace / GoDaddy / NicNames."

**Response:**
> "Absolutely — those are real options and they work for a lot of
> businesses. The difference is that builders give you tools and
> templates; you still have to figure out the structure, write the
> copy, decide what pages to create, and set up the estimate flow.
> What I provide is the structure, the strategy, and the implementation
> — done for you, specific to a local-service business. If you have
> the time and interest to build it yourself, a builder is a fine
> choice. If you would rather have it done properly and quickly,
> that is what I do."

---

## Objection 3: "I get enough work from referrals."

**Response:**
> "That is great — referrals are valuable. The site supports referrals
> rather than replacing them. When someone receives a referral from a
> friend, the first thing they do is search your name or check the link
> you share. A professional site means that check confirms you are the
> right choice, rather than raising doubts. It also means you are
> capturing work from people who search directly — which adds to the
> referral base over time."

---

## Objection 4: "I do not have many photos."

**Response:**
> "That is fine to start. We can build the service structure, service
> area pages, and estimate flow first — and use simple placeholder
> visuals or stock-style images in the meantime. As you complete more
> jobs, the photos get added. Even 5–10 real photos make a significant
> difference. Taking a few photos at the end of each job is a simple
> habit that builds the gallery over time."

---

## Objection 5: "I do not want to pay a lot."

**Response:**
> "There are different levels depending on what you need. A starter
> setup with a homepage, services page, and contact form is a lighter
> investment. You can add project examples, city pages, and automation
> later as the business grows. I would rather start with something
> that fits your budget and works well than oversell a package that
> does not make sense for where you are right now."

---

## Objection 6: "Will this guarantee my Google ranking?"

**Response:**
> "No — and anyone who guarantees a Google ranking is not being honest
> with you. What this gives you is a stronger local SEO foundation:
> organized service pages, city-specific landing pages, proper titles
> and descriptions, and a structure that search engines can read
> clearly. Rankings also depend on your Google Business Profile,
> the number and quality of reviews, backlinks, competition in your
> area, and how long the site has been active. The structure makes
> all of those other efforts more effective."

---

## Objection 7: "I do not want complicated AI."

**Response:**
> "Completely understood — and you do not need it. The automation
> can be as simple as: a form that sends you an email when someone
> submits a request, and a spreadsheet that logs the leads. That
> alone is already a significant improvement over a basic contact
> form. AI and chatbots are optional additions for businesses that
> want them — they are never required."

---

## Objection 8: "I am too busy to deal with this right now."

**Response:**
> "That makes sense — and it is actually a reason why having this
> set up matters. A structured site with a proper estimate form
> reduces the time you spend on back-and-forth messages before
> an estimate. Customers send what you need upfront. On the setup
> side, I handle the work — you mostly need to provide your business
> information, services, and photos. I can work with what you have
> now and fill in the rest as you get time."

---

## Objection 9: "I tried a website before and it didn't do anything."

**Response:**
> "That is a common experience — and usually it comes down to one
> of a few things: the structure was too generic, there were no
> city-specific pages, the request flow was too vague, or the site
> was launched and never promoted. A site does not generate leads
> by itself; it needs to be found and it needs to convert. I can
> look at what was there before and tell you specifically what was
> missing — that way you have a clearer picture before making
> any decision."

---

## Objection 10: "My customers find me on Craigslist / Facebook / Nextdoor."

**Response:**
> "Those channels work well for many local businesses. A website
> supports them rather than replacing them. When you post on
> Facebook or Nextdoor and someone wants to learn more, they look
> for a website or Google Business Profile. A professional site
> means that look confirms trust. It also means that customers who
> search directly on Google — not just on social platforms — can
> find you too."

## Notes for Ihor / Примітки для Іхора

> - Більшість заперечень — це не "ні", це "я не впевнений".
>   Твоя задача — дати інформацію, не переконувати силою.
>
> - Заперечення 3 ("і так є робота") — дуже поширене.
>   Не сперечайся. Погодься і поясни, що сайт підтримує
>   рекомендації, а не замінює їх.
>
> - Заперечення 6 (гарантія Google) — НІКОЛИ не обіцяй позиції.
>   Це не чесно і шкодить довірі. Говори тільки про "сильнішу основу".
>
> - Якщо клієнт каже "занадто дорого" — запропонуй Пакет 1
>   або розрахунок з оплатою в два етапи. Не знижуй ціну без причини.
>
> - Якщо клієнт каже "я подумаю" — запитай: "Що саме потрібно
>   прояснити, щоб прийняти рішення?" Часто є конкретне питання.
