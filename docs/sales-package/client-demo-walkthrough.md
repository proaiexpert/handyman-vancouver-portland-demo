# Client Demo Walkthrough

Use this script when showing the demo site to a potential client via screen
share, in person, or by sending the URL with a short description.

---

## Recommended walkthrough route

### 1. Homepage
**URL:** https://proai-expert.com/handyman-vancouver-portland-demo/

**What to show:**
- The first impression when someone lands on the site
- Large, clear headline — immediately tells visitors what the business does
- Amber call-to-action buttons — "Request Estimate" and "Send Photos"
- Service chips below the headline — fast scan of what is offered
- Hero photo — premium, professional feel
- Scrolling sections: services overview, work examples preview, service area, FAQ preview
- Footer with full sitemap and contact information

**What to say:**
> "This is the first thing a customer sees when they search for you online or
> someone sends them your link. Within the first few seconds, they know what
> you do, where you work, and how to get in touch. That clarity is what this
> structure is designed to create."

---

### 2. Services page
**URL:** https://proai-expert.com/handyman-vancouver-portland-demo/services/

**What to show:**
- Clean, numbered service categories
- Short descriptions — clear and scannable
- "What helps with the first review" section
- Examples section
- Scope note (some work may need a specialist)
- CTA at the bottom

**What to say:**
> "Instead of a long paragraph that says "we do everything," each service
> is listed clearly. This helps customers find what they need faster, and it

## Short walkthrough script (2–3 minutes)

Use this version when time is limited — a quick phone call, a Telegram
voice message, or a fast first impression.

> "I want to show you a sample website structure I built for a local
> handyman business. It has a homepage with a clear service summary, a
> services page, a project examples page, a service area section with
> individual city pages, a request estimate form, and a FAQ.
>
> Here is the link: https://proai-expert.com/handyman-vancouver-portland-demo/
>
> If you like the structure, I can adapt it for your business — your name,
> your services, your cities, your photos. It takes a few days to adapt,
> not weeks."

## Full walkthrough script (7–10 minutes)

Use this for screen share, an in-person meeting, or a detailed Telegram/email
explanation.

**Step 1 — Open the homepage.**
> "This is the first impression. Notice it is clean, professional, and
> immediate. The headline says what the business does. There are clear
> buttons to request an estimate or send photos. Below, you can scroll
> through the services, see example projects, and understand the service
> area. This is what a customer sees when they search for you or click
> your link."

**Step 2 — Navigate to Services.**
> "Services are listed clearly, not buried in a paragraph. Each one has a
> short explanation. This makes it easier for customers to find what they
> need, and it helps search engines understand what you offer."

**Step 3 — Navigate to Work Examples.**
> "This is where project photos go. In your version, these would be real
> photos from your jobs. Before and after photos are especially effective.
> Customers trust what they can see. Right now the demo uses sample
> scenarios — your version would use your actual work."

**Step 4 — Navigate to Service Area.**
> "This page shows the cities you serve. Each city also has its own page.
> That matters for local search. If someone in Beaverton searches for a
> flooring contractor, a page that specifically mentions Beaverton has a
> better chance of appearing."

**Step 5 — Click one city page.**
> "This is what a city-specific page looks like. It has a headline for
> that city, local notes about typical request types, a map reference,
> and a CTA. We would create pages like this for every city in your
> service area."

**Step 6 — Navigate to Request Estimate.**
> "This is not a basic contact form. It guides customers through what you
> actually need to review a job — service type, description, location,
> photos, and timing. That reduces the back-and-forth before you can
> give a price."

**Step 7 — Navigate to FAQ.**
> "This page answers common questions before a customer even asks them.
> It saves you from answering the same things over and over, and it
> builds trust before someone contacts you."

**Close:**
> "The demo is built for a handyman business in Vancouver, WA. For your
> business, everything would be changed — your name, your services, your
> cities, your real project photos. The structure is what matters, and
> the structure is already done."

## Russian notes for Ihor

> **Примітки для Іхора (не для клієнта):**
>
> - **Головна:** Покажи, що сайт виглядає професійно і зрозуміло з першої секунди.
>   Клієнт повинен зрозуміти — його сайт може виглядати так само.
>
> - **Послуги:** Акцент на структурі. Не "ми робимо все", а чітко і зрозуміло.
>   Це важливо для Google і для клієнта.
>
> - **Приклади робіт:** Скажи, що реальні фото замінять ці. Запитай, чи є у
>   клієнта фото з проєктів. Навіть 5-10 фото вже достатньо для початку.
>
> - **Сервісна зона і міста:** Це ключовий аргумент для локального SEO.
>   Поясни, що окремі сторінки для міст — це не просто красиво, це стратегія.
>
> - **Форма заявки:** Поясни, що структурована форма зменшує кількість
>   непотрібних питань перед оцінкою. Клієнт отримує відразу потрібну інформацію.
>
> - **FAQ:** Покажи як зручність для клієнта — менше питань, більше довіри.
>
> - Не продавай "технологію". Продавай **структуру і результат**.
>   "Ваш бізнес буде виглядати так само — з вашими послугами і фото."
