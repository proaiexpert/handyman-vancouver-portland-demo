Helpful Photos to Include

Good photos help clarify scope before any scheduling. Describe what you have or would take — photo delivery can be connected before launch.

📐

Wide View

Step back and capture the full wall, door, deck section, or room. Shows scale and surrounding context.

🔎

Close-Up

Get within 12 to 18 inches of the damage, crack, gap, or issue. Sharp focus helps assess material and depth.

🚪

Access

Show how the area is reached — doorway clearance, tight corners, exterior access, or anything that affects the work.

📏

Matching Area / Scale

Place a coin, tape measure, or your hand next to the damage. Include a matching surface if paint or material needs to blend.
