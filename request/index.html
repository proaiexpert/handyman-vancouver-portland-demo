Photo Checklist

Good photos help us understand scope before any scheduling. Send photos by text or email after submitting the form.

📐

Wide Shot — Full Area

Step back and capture the full wall, door, deck section, or room. Shows scale and surrounding context.

🔎

Close-Up — The Problem

Get within 12 to 18 inches of the damage, crack, gap, or issue. Sharp focus helps assess material and depth.

📏

Reference for Scale

Place a coin, tape measure, or your hand next to the damage so we can gauge actual size.

💡

Good Lighting

Natural light or a flashlight pointed at the area works best. Avoid flash-only shots that wash out detail.
