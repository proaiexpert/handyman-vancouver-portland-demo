Concept-site privacy

Privacy information.

This page explains the current non-operating, no-data-collection status of the Local Repair Pro concept website.

Concept status

Local Repair Pro is a production-like website concept created by ProAI Expert. It is not currently presented as an operating home-repair or handyman business.

Forms and personal information

The request form is a browser-only interface preview. Its submission is intercepted locally. The concept does not send, store or transmit the information entered into the form.

Do not enter sensitive personal, financial, medical, identity or access-control information into the concept form.

Phone and email placeholders

The phone number uses the reserved fictional 555-01xx range. The email address uses the reserved .example domain. They are included to demonstrate interface and link behavior and are not represented as operational contact channels.

Analytics, cookies and tracking

No analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral tracking or external form-processing service is intentionally included in the current repository implementation. Basic hosting infrastructure may still process ordinary technical request data according to the hosting provider’s own policies.

Images and external sources

Images used in this concept illustrate common project categories and are not presented as completed Local Repair Pro work. Rights and permitted use remain governed by the actual licenses or source terms associated with each asset.

Future changes

A real operating-business version would require an intentionally selected privacy policy, consent language, secure form endpoint, retention rules and verified business contact information before collecting customer data.

Questions

For questions about the concept project itself, use the ProAI Expert website rather than the fictional Local Repair Pro contact details.
