Concept-site terms

Terms of use.

These terms describe the limited purpose of the Local Repair Pro concept website. They are written for clarity and do not imply attorney review.

Purpose of the website

Local Repair Pro is a design, content, UX and front-end development concept created by ProAI Expert. It demonstrates how a premium local handyman or home-repair website could organize services, geographic context and estimate requests.

No active service business

The website does not represent a currently verified operating Local Repair Pro business. It does not offer or accept real repair services, bookings or project commitments.

No quote, contract or professional advice

Content about pricing, scope, permits and specialist work is general concept content. It is not a quote, contract, inspection, diagnosis, legal opinion or professional trade advice.

Form preview

The form does not send information. Completing it does not create a request, customer relationship, appointment or obligation for any person or business.

Fictional contact details

The 555-01xx phone number and .example email are reserved concept placeholders. They should not be used to contact or identify a real person or operating business.

Example scenarios and images

Project scenarios illustrate common categories. They are not represented as completed Local Repair Pro work, verified before-and-after results or evidence of operating experience.

Changes

The concept may be revised as design, content and technical QA continue. A future operating-business version would require separate verified terms based on the actual business, services and jurisdiction.
