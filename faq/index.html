COMMON QUESTIONS

Common questions before requesting an estimate

Photos, location, access, and project details help clarify scope and next steps.

How do I request an estimate?

Use our Request Estimate page to describe your project, share a few photos, and provide your preferred timeline. We review every submission and respond within one business day with a clear, no-surprise quote.

What kinds of projects do you handle?

We specialize in interior repairs and maintenance: drywall patching, door and trim adjustments, TV and shelf mounting, furniture assembly, minor plumbing fixes (like faucet swaps), and punch-list items. See our full Services page for the complete list.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Vancouver, WA and Portland, OR plus surrounding communities including Camas, Battle Ground, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Gresham, Tigard, Lake Oswego, and Oregon City. Visit our Service Area page for the full coverage map.

How long does it take to get a response?

We respond to all estimate requests within one business day. During busy seasons or for larger scopes, we may need an additional day to provide an accurate quote — but we always keep you informed.

Do you provide written estimates?

Yes. Every project gets a written estimate before work begins. We outline the scope, materials (if any), estimated time, and total cost. No work starts until you approve the estimate in writing.

What should I send with my estimate request?

Photos are the single most helpful thing you can share. Clear photos of the area to be repaired or improved let us give you an accurate quote without a preliminary site visit in most cases. Include a short description of what you want done and your preferred scheduling window.

Are there projects you don't take on?

We focus on handyman-scale work rather than full remodels, major electrical, or structural changes. If your project turns out to need a licensed specialist (e.g., panel upgrades or load-bearing modifications), we'll tell you upfront and can often point you to a trusted referral.

Before you request

Pre-estimate checklist

  • ✓Know the general service type (trim, wall, install, etc.)
  • ✓Have photos of the area ready (wide + close-up)
  • ✓Know your city or general area
  • ✓Note any access, entry, or parking details
  • ✓Group multiple small items into one request if possible
Why photos matter

Photos help clarify scope

A wide photo of the room and a close-up of the repair area help determine if the scope, materials, and access are a match before the next step is clarified.

Tip: text or email photos directly if the form upload is easier that way.
