FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions

How do I request an estimate?

Use the Request Estimate page to describe your project, share photos, and include your city or ZIP code. Requests are reviewed by project type, access, materials, and scope before the next step is clarified.

What kinds of projects do you handle?

Small repairs, installs, trim fixes, touch-ups, and punch-list projects. Common requests include wall repair and patching, door and trim adjustments, TV mounting, small fixture installs, washer/dryer connections, and grouped punch-list items. See the full Services page for categories.

Can I send photos before scheduling?

Yes. Sending photos before any scheduling helps clarify the scope, materials, and access situation before the next step is confirmed. Photos can be included with the estimate request form or sent directly.

Do you handle small punch-list projects?

Yes. Grouped punch-list items — multiple small repairs or installs submitted together — are one of the most common request types. Listing all items together with photos helps the review process.

Can multiple small tasks be grouped into one request?

Yes. Grouping several small tasks into one request is encouraged. Include photos of each area, a brief note about each item, and any relevant access or material details.

What details should I include in my request?

The most useful details are: photos of the repair or install area (wide shot + close-up), city or ZIP code, service type or description, rough timing, and any access or material notes. The more context provided, the clearer the next step will be.

What areas do you serve?

Vancouver, WA and Portland, OR plus surrounding communities including Camas, Battle Ground, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Gresham, Tigard, Lake Oswego, and Oregon City. Visit the Service Area page for the full coverage map. Availability depends on location, project type, access, and scope.

What happens after I send a request?

Requests are reviewed by project type, access, materials, location, and scope. A follow-up will clarify whether the request is a fit, what additional information may help, and what the next step looks like.

Is every request accepted?

Not every request is a fit. Availability depends on location, project type, scope, materials, and scheduling. The review process exists to clarify fit before any next step is confirmed — not every request will move forward.

How long does it take to get a response?

Most requests receive a follow-up within one business day. During busy periods or for more complex scope reviews, it may take an additional day to provide useful detail. Requests are not confirmed until reviewed.

What types of jobs may need a licensed trade specialist or contractor?

Some requests — including major electrical panel work, structural modifications, load-bearing changes, or certain plumbing work — may require a licensed trade specialist or contractor depending on scope, local rules, and permit requirements. If a project requires a specialist, the review will note that.

Are there projects you don’t take on?

Full remodels, major electrical or structural work, and projects that require a licensed contractor are outside the typical request scope. If a project requires a specialist, the review follow-up will clarify that and may point toward other options.

BEFORE YOU REQUEST

Pre-estimate checklist

  • Know the general service type
  • Have photos ready (wide + close-up)
  • Know your city or ZIP code
  • Note access, entry, or parking details
  • Group multiple items into one request
  • Note any material or timing constraints
WHY PHOTOS MATTER

Photos help clarify scope

A wide photo of the room and a close-up of the repair or install area help determine if scope, materials, and access are a fit before the next step is confirmed. Include both views when possible.

WHEN A SPECIALIST MAY BE NEEDED

Licensed trade work

Some requests may require a licensed trade specialist or contractor depending on scope, structure, electrical or plumbing requirements, or local rules. The review will flag this if relevant.

Ready to send details?

Share photos, your city, and a brief project description. Requests are reviewed by scope and location before the next step is clarified.
