ESTIMATE FACTORS

What affects the estimate

Each request is different. These are the main factors that shape what a repair or install request involves.

Service type

Wall, door, fixture, mounting, trim, or punch-list — the kind of work shapes what tools, time, and access are needed.

Materials

Whether materials are on hand, need to be sourced, or require a specific match affects the scope.

Access

Ground-floor, elevated, tight space, or outdoor access each changes what the visit involves.

Location

City and ZIP help confirm service area coverage and any local requirements that may apply.

Timing

Standard scheduling, flexible windows, or time-sensitive requests may each be handled differently.

Number of small tasks

Grouping several small items into one visit is often more efficient than booking separately.

Specialty requirements

Some requests may involve scope that needs a licensed trade specialist. That gets flagged during review.

REQUEST TYPES

Simple request categories

Most requests fall into one of these general categories. Scope and details are needed before any next step.

  • Small touch-ups
  • Install & mounting requests
  • Door, trim & hardware
  • Punch-list projects
  • Larger or specialist-reviewed work

WHAT TO SEND

What to include in your request

A complete request helps move things along faster and reduces back-and-forth.

  • Photos of the area or item
  • City and ZIP code
  • Service type (repair, install, punch-list)
  • Preferred timing or window
  • Access notes (stairs, gate, parking)
  • Known constraints or special requirements
