A useful request package

Five pieces of context.

You do not need technical construction language. Use clear observations and visible information.

1What needs attention

The issue and the desired result.

2Where it is

Room, exterior area, city or ZIP.

3What it looks like

Wide and close-up photos.

4What is involved

Materials, products and access.

5When it matters

Flexible, soon or tied to a date.

Plain language

Describe what you see, not what you think the trade diagnosis is.

“The door rubs at the top corner” is more useful than guessing that the frame needs replacement. Photos, movement and visible conditions support the review.
